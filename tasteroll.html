01 · What it is

Two products, one page

Chance is the dice. It supplies real entropy, minted once into this demo’s deck of legal rolls — order and ids frozen at mint time, no live service behind the curtain.

TasteCheck is the rules. It is an evidence-backed quality gate for AI-built interfaces: measured contrast, anti-slop audits, and craft rails a design cannot roll below.

tasteroll is where they meet. Constrained randomness you can feel: every click draws a new world from the deck, and every world is already legal.

This page is static. It draws from a frozen deck and never calls home — no Chance server, no analytics, no network at all.

02 · How the dice works

Roll. Lock. Re-roll.

  1. Roll

    Every visit starts on a random legal roll. The dice in the header rolls every dimension you have not locked.

  2. Lock

    Like the type but not the palette? Lock the dimension you want to keep. Locked dimensions never change again until you unlock them.

  3. Re-roll

    The deck walks without repeats and reshuffles when it runs out. The address bar always holds a link that reopens the current roll.

Roll ids look like r0483. They are mint-time ids from the Chance deck — say one out loud and someone else can open the exact same design.

03 · Quality floor

Randomness is not an excuse

Every roll in the deck sits inside the rails: readable measure and line-height, AA contrast computed for the surface you are looking at, one accent, and a banlist of tired tells — purple gradients, glass panels, pill buttons, the centered three-card hero.

  • Keyboard reachable, focus visible, skip link on top.
  • Reduced motion respected; every roll change is announced to screen readers.
  • No opt-out checkbox. The floor is the product.

The demo freezes a deck so a static page can show the whole space. The agent skill generates fresh faces per project — same rails, your context.
