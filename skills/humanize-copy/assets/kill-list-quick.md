# Humanize Copy — Quick Kill List

Paste-able cheat sheet. Replace each with a *specific*, not a synonym.

## Words to kill
delve · dive into · leverage · empower · foster · streamline · optimize · elevate ·
unleash · unlock · underscore · navigate · seamless · robust · cutting-edge ·
scalable · dynamic · comprehensive · transformative · revolutionary · game-changer ·
myriad · plethora · pivotal · multifaceted · landscape · tapestry · realm · beacon ·
journey · roadmap · symphony · ecosystem · testament · synergy · furthermore ·
moreover · additionally

## Phrases to kill
"In today's fast-paced / ever-evolving world" · "Let's dive in" · "It's not just X,
it's Y" · "Not only… but also" · "In conclusion" · "At the end of the day" · "Here's
the kicker" · "Real talk" · "Picture this" · "Imagine a world where" · "This aims to" ·
"It's important to note" · "It could be argued" · "Generally speaking"

## Rhythm/stance checks
- [ ] No 3+ sentences of the same length in a row
- [ ] Doesn't open with filler — starts on the point
- [ ] No hedging; takes an actual position
- [ ] Contains real specifics (numbers, names, details) — all verified
- [ ] Passes the pub test (say it aloud to a colleague)
- [ ] Not thesaurus-salad (plainer, not fancier)
- [ ] Couldn't appear verbatim on a competitor's site (has brand-specific detail)
