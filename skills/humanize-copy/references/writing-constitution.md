# Writing Constitution — portable floor

This repository-owned subset is the mandatory, portable information-architecture floor for
`humanize-copy`. It keeps the skill self-contained: an installed agent does not need a
workstation-local policy checkout. Apply these rules after the fact ledger is locked and before
the final self-check.

## Sentence

- Old before new: begin with the familiar topic, then put the new payload in the comment.
- Put the important or unfamiliar payload late enough to receive stress, without hiding the
  action or consequence.
- Make characters the subjects and actions the verbs. Use nominalizations only when they name a
  familiar object or link back to an established idea.

## Paragraph

- Anchor: elaborate one thing and repeat its topic.
- Linking: pick up the previous comment as the next topic and stay on one stage.
- Umbrella: name the super-category first, then rotate through its parts.
- Preview: announce the parts, then develop them in that order.
- Keep one related topic set and one thesis. Cohesion alone is not coherence.
- Echo key terms exactly when they carry the argument; do not vary them for elegance.

## Idea and audit

- Name the problem, acknowledge the concession, show the cost, then present the idea as the
  solution.
- Prefer concrete actions, images, and consequences over abstract meta-discourse.
- Audit each sentence by checking its first seven or eight words for the topic string, then check
  the end-to-start handoff, the paragraph pattern, and the repeated key terms.

Record the rule IDs used in evidence: `SENTENCE`, `PARAGRAPH`, `IDEA`, and `AUDIT`. These rules
govern structure and argument; the kill-list governs formulaic cadence. They are complementary,
not interchangeable.
