Fig. 01

There are two AI slops. We refuse both.

Ask for a site, you get the purple gradient. Ask for “not AI,” you get the cream-and-serif. Both are the average — one season apart.

The purple gradient

Indigo→violet, Inter, centered hero, three emoji cards, pill buttons. 2023–2025.

The editorial serif

Fraunces, cream paper, brick-red accent, oversized italics. The 2026 default.
