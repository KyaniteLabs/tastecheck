There are two AI slops.

/ we refuse both
✕ Slop v1

The purple gradient

Indigo→violet, Inter, centered hero, three emoji cards, pill buttons. 2023–2025.

✕ Slop v2

The editorial serif

Fraunces, cream paper, brick-red accent, oversized italics. The 2026 default.

It interviews you first.

/ how it works
01

It grills you

Function first, then references — it finds the pattern across what you love.

“‘Modern’ is a non-answer — name one you’d resemble.”
02

It commits

OKLCH palette with verified contrast, a real type pairing, structure, motion — as tokens.

“One dominant color, not five. And not indigo→violet.”
03

It builds & checks

The skills apply the system, then audit the result against your spec — not the average.

“That wine-red is the new tell. For your energy, push here.”
