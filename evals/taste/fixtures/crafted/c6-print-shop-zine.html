Twoinks.

Why two colors

Every job we take is printed in two spot inks on a risograph drum. The limit is the point: registration drifts, overlaps turn muddy in good ways, and nobody is tempted to add a third gradient.

Turnaround

Runs under 300 copies take nine days. We batch by ink, so if you share your color with another job the price drops and the wait grows by two.

Files

Send one grayscale PDF per ink, black meaning full coverage. If you do not know what that means, send what you have and we will separate it for a flat fee.

Paper

Uncoated stock only. The sample drawer is open on Fridays; take whatever you can fold.
