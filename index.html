01 / The 2026-09-05 retrofit

The gate had to earn its verdict.

A 7-wave gauntlet tested the kit. The external adversarial review then found holes in how the gate trusted evidence. The retrofit closes those holes.

External ASTRA review: 8 findings closed, including 5 SEV-1 evidence-authority holes. The five repairs below change what can support a verdict.

8 findings. 8 closed.Recorded in the ASTRA fix report. Closure does not upgrade public release status.

  1. Verdicts now DERIVED from observations.

    The gate derives the result from recorded observations. A declared pass cannot stand in for the work.

  2. Reviews bound to check + artifact + evidence.

    A review carries the check ID, rubric, artifact digest, and complete-evidence digest. A transplanted approval produces HOLD.

  3. Dependency-manifest capture.

    The capture includes linked local CSS, JavaScript, and fonts. Changed dependencies block release even when the entry file is unchanged.

  4. Subject inventories.

    Browser coverage reconciles route × state × viewport. Control coverage reconciles control × state. A smaller claimed scope cannot erase required subjects.

  5. Complete-capture hashing.

    The complete evidence gets hashed. Lossy or truncated captures cannot pass; redaction belongs to presentation.

The other three findings separate verifier and consumer roots, distinguish craft floors from style defaults, and keep authority claims scoped to the evidence.
