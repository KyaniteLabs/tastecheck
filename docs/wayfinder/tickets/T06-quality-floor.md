# Quality floor

## Question

What guarantees every roll is high quality?

## Resolution

**Always on, no opt-out** (this page *is* the demo):

1. Design rails (`design-rails.json` bounds)
2. Anti-slop banlist (purple gradient, glass, pill-999, centered-3-card hero, etc.)
3. A11y invariants on controls **and** rolled surface (skip/main/focus-visible/reduced-motion/keyboard; contrast floors)

No “strict a11y” checkbox. Quality is not optional on the proof surface.
