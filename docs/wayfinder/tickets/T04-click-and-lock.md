# Click and lock model

## Question

What does one dice click do, and how do locks work?

## Resolution

- **Default:** full re-roll of all **unlocked** dimensions.
- **Optional locks:** pin any of ref/vibe/mode/sig; re-roll only unlocked.
- **Deck walk:** shuffled inventory per visit; when empty, reshuffle.
- **Shareable:** roll id/seed in header and URL.
