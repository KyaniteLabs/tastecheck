# What the dice may change

## Question

On each roll, what layers may change vs stay fixed?

## Resolution

**May change (A+B):** visual system (color, type, density feel, radius, borders, accent moves, light/dark posture) **and** layout structure (hero shape, columns, rails, section rhythm, signature structures).

**Must not change:** product story, section inventory (IA), nav destinations, interactive behaviors, a11y/quality floor.

**Not in the dice:** copy rewrites (C) or product identity swaps (D).
