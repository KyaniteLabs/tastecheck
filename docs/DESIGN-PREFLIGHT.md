# TasteCheck design preflight

This is the DECREE-003 receipt. Write it before any icon, logo or visual-identity asset.

## 1. Purpose

**User outcome:** a coding agent's UI stops looking AI-built and ships only when a gate says SHIP with evidence (README, v1.7.0).

The mark has to carry *judgement*: an honest yes or no, not decoration.

## 3. Rejected metaphors

- An eye, a pupil or tiger stripes. This is stone-lock law: tiger's eye is a material, not a motif.
- Blue apatite or cyan crystal. That is DialectOS's stone, and r15 used it by mistake.
- Any r15-style "halfway" loupe variant. Simon, 2026-11-10: "they all look the same". The next round must change form and composition, not just tune the material.
- Sparkles, glints, orange or pink flecks inside a lens. Simon rejected R21 for exactly this.
- A literal checkmark badge or a shield. That is generic SaaS template slop.
- Any glyph on a die or stamp face. In r17 a "tick relief" rendered as a "/" (which reads as "prohibited"), a "T", or a chart line. Die faces stay plain.
- r20 seal stamp and hallmark punch. Simon, 2026-11-10: "too different". New objects drift away from the mark he liked.
- r17 losers: the loupe (still reads as a lens on a stick), the tastevin (the stone covers only 12% of it, and it reads as a frying pan), and the go/no-go gauge (13% coverage, which reads as a pen at 32 px).

## 4. Material reference

- **Habit:** dense honey-gold to deep brown body. Golden and dark-brown bands alternate *across* the piece, and inside each band fine fibres run perpendicular to the band edges. The chatoyant sheen lights one band while its neighbour falls dark (Smithsonian GeoGallery specimen; Science News, "Eye of the Tiger"). Not rust, leather, wood, amber or faceted gem.
- **r17 correction (paired audit, 2026-11-10):** the r17 prompt asked for fibres and the light band running *along* the handle. That is wood-grain and brushed-metal geometry, and all 10 renders read as orange wood or copper, at a measured hue of 21–25° and saturation of about 0.9. Target: median hue at least 33° and saturation at most 0.7. *(Superseded after r19: #C08A2E is itself saturation 0.76, so the saturation cap was wrong. Use raw colour near the two target swatches, with no global grade.)*
- **r19 correction:** at 32 px, regular bands read as yellow-and-black stripes. Use one dominant gold body with at most two thin, irregular brown seams, dark area at most 25%, gold-to-dark contrast at most 2.5:1, judged at 32 px.
- **Family renders of the same stone:** TasteCheck R21 master (PR #105). Its handle is right; its lens sparkles are rejected. Earlier sittings: `kanban/attachments/t_28dbdbfb/SITTING-1-TASTECHECK-V11-R2.png`, `t_2fc42fd6/tastecheck-r8-*`.

## 5. Family icon law and form proposal

**Family construction:** one hero object at about 80% fill, a three-quarter view from about 20° above, a key light from the upper left, dark brushed gunmetal with narrow cold-silver catches, and real mineral substance. No glow, flare, sparkle, cast shadow or text. The background is flat for keying.

**Current direction (Simon, 2026-11-10, after r20):** the seal and punch were "too different". Go back to the R21 loupe and keep its rim, iris lens, handle, pose and proportions. Change only the light: no sparkles, no rainbow glints. In r22 Simon called candidate A (`tc22-clean-v1`) "closest". This supersedes the r15 "change the form" note and the r17 loupe rejection, because both of those were judged against r15-style variants, not against R21.

**Round r17 concepts (superseded).** Each one is a different object, and each carries a tiger's-eye handle:

| ID | Object | Why it fits TasteCheck |
|---|---|---|
| A | Go/no-go plug gauge (two-ended machinist gauge) | The literal binary gate: one end passes, the other must not |
| B | Hallmark punch (assayer's steel stamp) | Assay: you mark the metal only after it has been tested |
| C | Tasting spoon (sommelier's tastevin) | *Taste* check: a small cup for judging |
| D | Jeweller's loupe, folding, closed-case | Inspection. It differs from r15 in form (a pivoting case, not a magnifier on a stick) |
| E | Wax-seal stamp | The sign-off seal: nothing leaves without it |

**Assumption:** "handle material" means the tiger's eye is the part you hold. It is not a lens, a body or an inlay.
